Transparency Promise

We believe support should be honest. HUGS is being built from the ground up, and every contribution helps us keep going.

  • Website hosting, domain, tools, design, and maintenance.
  • Building and improving the Send a Hug digital card system.
  • Creating resource pages for people facing hard times.
  • Developing HUGS art, gallery, outreach, and community projects.
  • Supporting the time, labor, and creative work needed to keep HUGS alive.
  • Future outreach efforts connected to food, clothing, shelter resources, and community help.
