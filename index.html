Transparency Note

HUGS is a growing community project. Contributions support project development, outreach, resources, website improvements, creative work, and real world help efforts.

  • Support is used to help build and maintain HUGS projects.
  • Support may help fund outreach materials, website tools, and community resources.
  • HUGS is not claiming contributions are tax-deductible unless officially operating through a registered nonprofit entity.
  • Our goal is to grow with honesty, service, creativity, and community trust.
